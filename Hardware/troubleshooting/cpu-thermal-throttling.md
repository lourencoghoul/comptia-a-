## Case 3: System Slow Due to Thermal Throttling

### Problem
User reports that the computer is very slow, even for simple tasks.

### Analysis
- CPU usage was low, but performance was poor  
- Monitored CPU temperature: constantly high (85°C+)  
- Detected thermal throttling  

### Action Taken
- Checked cooling system  
- Found that cooler was not properly secured  
- Reinstalled cooler correctly  
- Cleaned system and improved airflow  

### Result
- Temperature reduced significantly  
- Performance returned to normal  

### Conclusion
The issue was caused by thermal throttling due to improper cooling installation.
